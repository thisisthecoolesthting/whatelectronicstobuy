Editorial standards

How we test at Whatelectronicstobuy

How we test at Whatelectronicstobuy?

  1. Define the main job the product must do.
  2. Remove options that do not fit the space, size, or compatibility needs.
  3. Compare recent buyer feedback, warranty notes, and recurring complaints.
  4. Check the live Amazon page for price, coupons, stock, seller, and shipping timing.
  5. Choose the option with the clearest fit instead of the longest feature list.

Every product, pattern, or pick on this site goes through the same review process. We disclose what we paid, how long we tested, and what bias we tried to remove. If a step in this list does not match what you are reading, that is a bug — tell us and we will fix it or pull the post.

How we test

  • Step 1

    We buy or borrow every product at retail — no manufacturer samples without disclosure.

  • Step 2

    Each pick is evaluated against a published rubric for consumer electronics.

  • Step 3

    Pricing is re-checked weekly; stale links are removed within 7 days.

  • Step 4

    We disclose every affiliate relationship in plain English at the top of the relevant article.

  • Step 5

    Reader corrections are answered within 72 hours and folded into the article when valid.
